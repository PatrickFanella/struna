First notes: pick the open G string, let it fade, count four slow beats and repeat.
Returning player: pick open D, G, B, G, one note per count.
Rhythm: gently mute the strings and brush down on counts 1, 2, 3, 4.
Returning player: count 1-and-2-and-3-and-4-and; down on each number,
up on the and after 2 and 4, silence on the other ands.
Chords: E minor uses fret 2 on the A and D strings, with other strings open.
Returning player: alternate four slow counts of E minor and A minor.

Try for about two minutes or stop sooner. Rest if the hand tightens; stop for pain.
Without a guitar, trace the pattern and count aloud. Exploration and playing are
recorded separately, and completion is the player's report, not an assessed result.
